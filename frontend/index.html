WHY MULTIPLE SIGNALS?

One clue is rarely enough.

A browser setting can be unusual for a perfectly valid reason. A person may use a keyboard, a touchscreen, or accessibility software.

Pointer movement adds context: how far the pointer traveled, how quickly it moved, and how often it paused.

Timing adds another view. Click intervals, idle periods, and scrolling help describe the shape of a session.

A useful assessment keeps these signals separate, explains the evidence, and acknowledges when it has too little information.

The demo uses baseline rules. It does not prove that a visitor is human, and it does not claim validated detection accuracy.
